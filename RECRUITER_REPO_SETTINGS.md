## Rename constraints

- `demo-repo`: rename only after the active AgentShield experiment/evidence line is frozen and verified.
- generated-suffix canonical repos: rename only after deployment URLs, CI, Lovable/Supabase references, external links and repository-family lineage are checked.
- typo-heavy academic/legacy names may be corrected after reference impact is verified.
